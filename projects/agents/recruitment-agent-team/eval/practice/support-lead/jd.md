# Customer Support Team Lead (B2B SaaS)

Panel: support

Ledgerly makes invoicing software for small agencies. The support team answers about 3,000 tickets a month
by email and chat.

## Must have
- 2+ years leading, managing or coaching a customer support team
- Hands-on use of a ticketing tool such as Zendesk, Freshdesk or Intercom
- Owning support metrics (CSAT, first response time, resolution time) and improving them
- Writing or maintaining help-center / knowledge-base articles

## Nice to have
- B2B SaaS support experience
- Spreadsheet or SQL analysis of ticket data
- Hiring and onboarding new support agents
